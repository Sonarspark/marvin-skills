## Evaluation Criteria

1. **Overall Architecture**: Is the 33-document collection organized logically? Are there natural groupings that should be made explicit (e.g., folders, sections, a table of contents)?
2. **Reading Paths**: Can a reader find what they need without reading everything? Are there clear entry points for different reader goals (portfolio construction vs. macro understanding vs. scenario analysis)?
3. **Document Scope**: Does each document have a clear, focused purpose? Are any documents trying to do too much, or too little? Should any be merged or split?
4. **Naming & Numbering**: Does the numbering scheme serve the reader? Would thematic grouping (with sub-numbering or folders) work better than a flat numbered list?
5. **Navigation**: Are cross-references sufficient? Can a reader follow a thread across documents without getting lost?
6. **Redundancy**: Is content unnecessarily duplicated across documents? Where it is repeated, is it intentional (reinforcement) or accidental?
7. **Progressive Disclosure**: Does the collection build knowledge progressively? Could a reader start at any point, or must they read sequentially?
8. **Summary Effectiveness**: Does 00_consolidated_summary.md serve as an effective entry point and roadmap?

## Output Notes

- Propose a specific reorganization scheme if the current flat structure can be improved
- Identify documents that could be merged, split, or resequenced
- Suggest a reading guide or table of contents structure
- Grade the current organization A-F and the proposed organization separately
