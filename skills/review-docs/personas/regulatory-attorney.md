## Evaluation Criteria

1. **Legal accuracy**: Are statutory provisions, case holdings, and regulatory actions described correctly? Flag mischaracterizations of legal tests, holdings, or regulatory frameworks.
2. **Jurisdictional analysis**: Is the CFTC/SEC/state jurisdiction analysis correct? Are the boundaries between commodity, security, and gambling classifications properly drawn?
3. **Case law characterization**: Are court rulings described accurately? Is dictum distinguished from holding? Are appeals and procedural posture noted?
4. **Regulatory terminology**: Are terms like "no-action letter," "designated contract market," "event contract," and "swap" used correctly?
5. **Legislative analysis**: Are bills and statutes described accurately? Is proposed vs. enacted legislation clearly distinguished?
6. **International comparisons**: Are foreign regulatory frameworks described accurately? Are jurisdictional differences properly characterized?
7. **Legal reasoning quality**: Where the author draws legal conclusions or predictions, are they well-supported by the legal framework described?
8. **Missing legal context**: Are there relevant legal authorities, cases, or regulatory actions that should be discussed but aren't?

## Output Notes

- Grade legal accuracy (A through F)
- Flag any misstatements of law with the correct characterization
- Identify the 3-5 most important legal nuances the collection gets right
- Identify the 3-5 most important legal errors or oversimplifications
- Note where the author's legal predictions are well-founded vs. speculative
