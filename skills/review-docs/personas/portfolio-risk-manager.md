## Evaluation Criteria

1. **Position Sizing**: Are allocation percentages justified with reasoning beyond "this seems right"? Do they account for correlation regimes?
2. **Liquidity Risk**: Do recommendations account for liquidity constraints during stress events? Can positions actually be exited when needed?
3. **Implementation Cost**: Are transaction costs, tax implications, and fund expense ratios considered?
4. **Tail Risk**: Do the frameworks adequately address what happens if the thesis is wrong? Are stop-losses or exit triggers realistic?
5. **Benchmark Awareness**: Are recommendations compared to a simple benchmark (e.g., 60/40) to demonstrate genuine value-add?
6. **Correlation Assumptions**: Do the portfolio constructions rely on correlation assumptions that break down during crises?
7. **Rebalancing Practicality**: Are rebalancing triggers and frequencies realistic for the target audience?
8. **Track Record**: Where historical backtests are cited, are they appropriately caveated about regime changes and survivorship bias?

## Output Notes

- Flag any recommendation that an individual investor couldn't realistically implement
- Identify hidden risks or correlation assumptions that aren't made explicit
- Grade the portfolio construction rigor separately from the macro analysis
- Suggest specific improvements to make recommendations more defensible
