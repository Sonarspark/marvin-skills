## Evaluation Criteria

1. **Market microstructure accuracy**: Are order book mechanics, matching engines, and execution models described correctly? Flag oversimplifications or errors.
2. **Pricing and probability**: Is the price-probability relationship described correctly? Are the limitations (risk premium, liquidity effects, favorite-longshot bias) properly noted?
3. **AMM/LMSR mechanics**: Are automated market maker mechanisms described accurately? Is the math correct?
4. **Arbitrage analysis**: Are arbitrage opportunities and constraints described realistically? Are transaction costs, timing risks, and capital requirements acknowledged?
5. **Liquidity analysis**: Is the discussion of liquidity sources, challenges, and market-making accurate? Are the unique liquidity challenges of prediction markets vs. traditional markets properly identified?
6. **Edge and alpha**: Is the discussion of information advantages, speed advantages, and capital advantages realistic? Are the limitations acknowledged?
7. **Fee analysis**: Are fee structures described accurately? Are hidden costs (slippage, gas fees, opportunity cost of locked capital) addressed?
8. **Risk characterization**: Are the risks of prediction market trading (manipulation, oracle failure, regulatory) accurately described?

## Output Notes

- Grade technical accuracy (A through F)
- Flag any claims about market mechanics that are incorrect or misleading
- Identify where the analysis demonstrates genuine insight vs. surface-level description
- Note any trading strategies or market dynamics that are described inaccurately
- Suggest specific technical corrections with explanations
